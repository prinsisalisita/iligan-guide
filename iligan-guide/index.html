Getting There

Getting to and around Iligan.

✈️🚌 Getting to Iligan

  • Nearest major airport: Laguindingan (Cagayan de Oro), then a bus or van to Iligan (roughly 1.5–2.5 hrs).
  • Regular buses and vans run along the coastal highway from Cagayan de Oro.
  • Buses also connect from Ozamiz and other Northern Mindanao cities.

🚐 Around the City

  • Jeepneys: cheapest, run set routes. Ask the driver or a local for your stop.
  • Tricycles: short trips; agree on the fare first.
  • Tartanilya: horse-drawn carriages still run in parts of the city, such as Tambacan and near the Pala-o market. Agree on the fare first.
  • Vans / taxis / hired rides: for waterfall day trips.

🥾 To the Falls

  • Maria Cristina and Timoga are easiest to reach from the city.
  • Tinago and Mimbalot need a ride plus a short descent by stairs or path.
  • Limunsudan is remote: go with a guide and arrange transport ahead.

Fares change. Ask your accommodation or the tourism office for current rates.

When to Go & Events

🌦️ Weather

Rain can arrive quickly at any time of year and raises river levels. Check the forecast the day before and go early for clearer falls and cooler treks.

🎉 Festivals

The Diyandi Festival honors St. Michael, the city's patron, around late September. Confirm exact dates and street closures with the tourism office.

Safety

🌧️ Weather & Water

  • Avoid falls and rivers after heavy rain; water can rise fast.
  • Swim only in marked areas, and wear a life vest if offered.
  • Wear shoes with good grip; rocks are slippery.

🎒 Travel Smart

  • Hire accredited local guides for treks.
  • Carry water, cash in small bills, a rain cover and sunscreen.
  • Keep valuables secure and tell someone your plan.

📞 Emergencies

  • National emergency hotline: 911.
  • Police, fire, hospital & DRRMO numbers are being verified. Ask the tourism office.
  • Check current travel advisories before your trip.

🤝 Respectful Travel

  • Dress modestly in churches and mosques.
  • Ask before photographing people.
  • Take your trash with you and protect the falls.

Food & Products

🍽️ Try

  • Fresh seafood and kinilaw
  • Iligan lechon with Pinakurat, the city's signature spiced vinegar
  • Halang-halang, a spicy chicken soup
  • Grilled favorites (sinugba)
  • Mindanao dishes such as pastil and Maranao piaparan
  • Local coffee, kakanin and tropical fruit

🛍️ Bring Home

  • Maranao malong and woven textiles
  • Handicrafts and brassware from the region
  • Local snacks and delicacies as pasalubong
